Snowball or Comeback? Early Objectives in Pro League of Legends

12,415 professional matches · 2022 season

Does taking the first dragon actually help a team win, or is it just a sign the team was already ahead?

  • In games that were close on gold at 15 minutes, first-dragon teams still won 56.6% vs 43.4% (permutation test, p < 0.0001).
  • A win model using only 15-minute data reaches about 75% accuracy.
  • A fairness test found no difference between major and minor leagues (p = 0.91).

Python · pandas · scikit-learn · hypothesis testing
